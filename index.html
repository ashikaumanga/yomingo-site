Comprehensive Learning Journey

📰 Read Real Japanese

Browse authentic news articles written for native speakers. Tap any word for instant dictionary lookup with furigana reading guides.

Our advanced Compound Word Breakdown technology analyzes complex Japanese terms, splitting them into easily understandable sub-components.

One tap saves words to your personal deck for practice later. Learn vocabulary in the context it's actually used.

🔍 Standalone Dictionary & Organization

Not just a reader—YominGo features a professional-grade standalone Japanese dictionary with Romaji support and intelligent multi-factor ranking.

Build User-Created Collections to neatly organize your vocabulary, and effortlessly bookmark your favorite articles for future reading.

🎴 Smart Flashcards

Powered by the FSRS algorithm (the science behind Anki), cards appear exactly when you're about to forget them.

Experience Sense-Aware Flashcards: reviews and exercises are tied to the specific contextual meaning you found the word in.

Progressive difficulty: Kanji → Reading → Meaning. Filter by JLPT level (N5-N1) to focus on what matters for your goals.

The real magic: Write Japanese sentences using your vocabulary, get detailed AI feedback on what you did right and what to improve.

Not just "correct" or "wrong"—get scored feedback on grammar, word choice, and natural usage. This is how you actually learn to use words, not just recognize them.
