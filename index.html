🎴 YominGo Smart Cards™

The feature that sets us apart from every other app.

Smart Cards combine FSRS spaced repetition with AI-graded writing practice in a single review flow:

  • FSRS scheduling (when to review)
  • You type sentences using the target word
  • AI evaluates: correct word usage + grammar + natural flow
  • Detailed feedback and scoring

Standalone Dictionary

Not just a reader—YominGo features a professional-grade standalone Japanese dictionary with Romaji support and intelligent multi-factor ranking.

Select text in other apps and instantly open YominGo Dictionary. Perfect for reading articles in Chrome or any browser—easily add words to your Collections without leaving your workflow.

Collection-based Organization

Build User-Created Collections organically by saving words as you read articles—every tap adds vocabulary in the context it appeared.

Or prepopulate a Collection instantly from the dictionary using existing tags like JLPT, Common, or Tech.
